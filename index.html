How an order runs.

  1. Message

    You describe the job on Fiverr. I reply with the package that fits and anything I need from you.

  2. Order

    You order and send your texts, logo or files. Payment stays with Fiverr until you accept the delivery.

  3. Preview

    You get a live preview link to review on your own phone and computer.

  4. Delivery

    After your revisions, you receive the files, the deployment, and a short guide to edit them.

Questions buyers ask first.

Can you write the texts for me?

Yes, in English or French. Mention it in your first message so it is included in the quote.

Do you use WordPress?

No. Static sites load faster, cost less to host, and have nothing to update or patch. If you need to edit texts often, I can set up a simple way to do it.

Where will my site be hosted?

On your own hosting if you have one, or on a free static host such as Vercel or Netlify, set up on your account so you stay in control.

Do I own the code?

Yes, entirely. You receive all source files with the delivery.

What can’t you automate?

Anything that breaks a website’s terms of use, sends unsolicited messages, or runs fake accounts.
